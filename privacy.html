Privacy Policy

Aapke documents aur jaankari ke saath hum kya karte hain — saaf shabdon mein.

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This policy explains how Canjain Global Advisors (CGA India) and this website, neerajjain.in, handle information. We are a professional advisory firm providing tax, legal and compliance services, and client confidentiality is central to how we work.

Who we are

Canjain Global Advisors, founded and led by Neeraj Jain, operates from Safidon (Haryana) and Burari (Delhi) and serves clients across India. This website is the personal and professional site associated with the firm.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need in order to respond to you or deliver a service you have asked for:

  • Contact details you give us — such as your name, phone number, email address and business name, when you contact us through this site, WhatsApp, email or a contact form.
  • Information you send for a professional engagement — such as documents, notices, statements and identifiers required to carry out the work you have engaged us for.
  • Basic technical data — such as pages visited and general usage information, collected by our hosting provider to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and explain what a matter involves.
  • To carry out the professional services you have engaged us for.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional obligations that apply to our practice.
  • To send you updates about your own matter. We do not add enquiry contacts to unrelated marketing lists.

Social media connections

Content published under the Bizwith Neeraj name may be posted to LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram through authorised applications connected to our own accounts. These connections allow us to publish our own posts. They do not read, collect or store data belonging to visitors of this site or to anyone who views our content.

AI chat assistant

This site includes an AI chat assistant called Aarzoo. If you use it, the message you type and the recent messages in that conversation are sent to our automation platform and to an AI model provider so that a reply can be generated. Those messages are used only to answer you — they are not used to build a profile of you and they are not added to any marketing list.

Aarzoo provides general information about our firm and services only. It does not give professional advice and it is not a substitute for speaking to us about your own matter. Please do not enter PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, passwords, OTPs or document numbers into the chat — send those to us directly instead. If you use the WhatsApp button shown in the chat, your message opens in WhatsApp and is then handled like any other WhatsApp enquiry.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is disclosed only:

  • to the tax, regulatory or judicial authority a matter is being filed with, on your instruction;
  • to service providers who help us operate (for example, hosting and communication tools), limited to what they need;
  • where disclosure is required by law or by the professional standards that govern our practice.

Confidentiality

Information you share with us in the course of a professional engagement is treated as confidential and is handled in accordance with the professional and ethical obligations applicable to Chartered Accountants and Advocates in India.

Keeping information

Records connected to a professional engagement are retained for the period required under applicable tax, corporate and professional record-keeping rules. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information in our care, including restricting access within our team to those working on your matter. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to stop contacting you. Write to us using the details below and we will respond. Some records may need to be retained where law or professional rules require it.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses cookies that are necessary for it to work and for basic usage measurement.

We also use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (Facebook and Instagram). It records that a browser visited a page on this site and reports that to Meta, so that we can see whether our ads are working and show ads to people who have visited us before. It does not tell Meta your name, your PAN, your documents or anything you have shared with us — only that a visit happened, together with the ordinary technical details a browser sends, such as IP address and the page address. Once that data reaches Meta, Meta’s own privacy policy applies to it as well.

If you would rather not be included, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, turn on your browser’s tracking protection, or change your ad preferences inside your Facebook or Instagram account. The site works normally either way.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
