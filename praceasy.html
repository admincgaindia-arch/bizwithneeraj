PracEasy: jo software mujhe khud chahiye tha

Ek practice chalate hue pata chalta hai ki ghante kahan jaate hain. PracEasy wahin se shuru hua.

Problem

Kaam kam nahi tha, bikhra hua tha

CGA mein 20 se zyada log hain aur hazaaron clients. Client ki details ek jagah, kaunsa kaam kiske paas hai doosri jagah, aur deadlines kisi ki diary mein. Har mahine yahi daud: kaunsi file ruki hai, kaunsa return baaki hai.

Market ke software ya to bahut generic the, ya kisi aisi company ke banaye hue jisne kabhi practice chalayi hi nahi.

Kya karta hai

Clients, kaam aur deadlines ek system mein

  • Har client ki jaankari ek jagah, taaki kisi file ke liye kisi se poochhna na pade.
  • Kaam assign karna, track karna aur review karna, partner ko poori tasveer aur team ko saaf zimmedari.
  • Recurring compliance aur deadlines system ki reedh hain, baad mein jodi gayi cheez nahi.

PracEasy pehle CGA ke andar chala aur sudhra, uske baad doosri firms ke liye khula.
