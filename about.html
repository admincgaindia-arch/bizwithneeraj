Kyun

Client do baar pay kyun kare?

Practice ke shuruaati saalon mein ek cheez baar baar dikhti thi. Accountant return file karta hai, notice aata hai, phir client ek lawyer dhoondhta hai jise poori kahani shuru se samjhani padti hai. Fees do jagah jaati hai, aur file ki samajh kisi ek ke paas bhi poori nahi hoti.

Isliye maine aisi practice banayi jahan tax aur legal kaam ek hi desk par hota hai. Jo team return banati hai, wahi notice ka jawab samajhti hai, aur zaroorat pade to wahi file aage appeal tak jaati hai.
