Aapke sawal

Jo sab poochte hain

Meri deadline nikal chuki hai — ab kuch ho sakta hai?
Aam taur par haan, lekin option kam ho jaate hain. Reply ki date nikli hai par order nahi aaya — toh late reply ke saath condonation request ban sakti hai. Order (DRC-07) aa gaya hai — toh appeal ka rasta hai, 3 mahine ke andar. Jo bhi stage ho, notice bhejiye — hum seedha bata denge ki abhi kya bacha hai aur kya nahi. Jhoothi umeed nahi denge.
Aapko kya documents chahiye?
Shuru karne ke liye sirf notice ki photo ya PDF. Uske baad notice ke hisaab se — GST portal login, relevant period ki GSTR-1/3B, purchase register, aur jis point par objection hai us se related invoices. Poori checklist hum pehle hi ek baar mein bhej dete hain.
Mujhe Safidon ya Delhi office aana padega?
Nahi. Notice reply ka poora kaam online hota hai — documents WhatsApp/email par, baat call par, filing hum karte hain. Sirf personal hearing wale matters mein physical presence ki baat aati hai, aur wahan bhi aam taur par hum representation karte hain. Hum Haryana, Delhi, UP, Rajasthan, Punjab regularly serve karte hain aur PAN India available hain.
48 ghante ka clock kab shuru hota hai?
Complete documents milne se — notice bhejne se nahi. Draft 24 ghante mein aapke review ke liye aata hai, aur aapke approval ke baad filing. Agar aapke records adhoore hain ya reconciliation heavy hai, toh hum pehle hi realistic timeline bata dete hain — deadline ke chakkar mein jhootha vaada nahi karte.
Reply file hone ke baad kya hota hai?
Teen mein se kuch ek: matter drop ho jaata hai (achhe reply ke baad yahi sabse common hai), department further clarification maangta hai, ya personal hearing lagti hai. Teeno situation mein aapko pata rahega kya expect karna hai — aur Tier 3 mein hum order tak saath rehte hain.
Mera notice income tax ka hai, GST ka nahi.
Wo bhi hum handle karte hain — 143(1) intimation se 148 reassessment tak. Notice bhej dijiye, section dekh ke bata denge. Income tax notices par ek guide bhi hai: ITR notice guide padhiye.
