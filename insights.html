Notes

Compliance ko kharcha mat samjhiye

Zyadatar business owners compliance ko ek bill ki tarah dekhte hain jo har mahine bharna padta hai. Par jab bank loan ke liye teen saal ke returns maangta hai, ya koi investor books dekhta hai, ya department notice bhejta hai, tab wahi purani filings aapki sabse badi taakat banti hain. Jo business apni compliance saaf rakhta hai, woh mauke aane par taiyaar milta hai.

Notice aaye to pehla kaam: ghabraaiye mat, padhiye

Har notice ka matlab penalty nahi hota. Kai baar department sirf kisi mismatch ki wajah poochh raha hota hai. Pehle notice poora padhiye, us par likhi jawab ki aakhri tareekh note kariye, aur jis period ki baat hai uske documents ikattha kariye. Jaldbaazi mein portal par kuch bhi submit mat kariye. Galat jawab, der se diye jawab se zyada nuksaan kar sakta hai.

File ek haath mein kyun rahe

Jab return ek jagah bane, notice ka jawab doosri jagah, aur appeal teesri jagah, to har baar kahani shuru se sunani padti hai aur har baar kuch chhoot jaata hai. Ek team jo shuru se file jaanti hai, woh wahi cheez pakad leti hai jo naya aadmi miss kar deta hai.

Books mahine ke mahine band kariye

Saal ke aakhir mein poore saal ki entries ek saath karna sabse mehenga tareeka hai. Bill kho jaate hain, bank entries ka matlab yaad nahi rehta, aur jo galti teen mahine pehle pakdi ja sakti thi woh seedhe return mein chali jaati hai. Har mahine books band ho jaayein to GST, TDS aur ITR teeno ek hi saaf data se bante hain, aur notice aaye to jawab dhoondhna nahi padta.

Business ka paisa, ghar ka paisa: do alag account

Ek hi bank account se kiraya, school fees aur supplier ki payment, yeh chhote business ki sabse aam aadat hai. Kaam chal jaata hai, jab tak koi poochhta nahi. Jab department ya bank poochhta hai ki yeh entry kya thi, tab har entry ki kahani sunani padti hai. Business ke liye alag account, aur usse ghar ke liye ek tay rakam nikaalna, yeh ek aadat aage ke kai sawaal pehle hi khatam kar deti hai.

Kaam shuru hone se pehle scope likhit mein

Fees ka jhagda aksar paise ka nahi, ummeed ka hota hai. Client ne socha tha notice ka jawab bhi isi mein shaamil hai, team ne socha tha sirf return. Isliye CGA mein kaam shuru hone se pehle likh diya jaata hai ki kya shaamil hai aur kya nahi. Do line ka yeh kaam baad ki lambi behas bacha leta hai, dono taraf.

CA firm bhi ek business hai

Jo firm doosron ke business ko system deti hai, uska apna kaam aksar diary, WhatsApp aur yaadasht par chalta hai. Due date kisi ek insaan ke dimaag mein ho, to woh chutti par jaaye aur deadline nikal jaaye. PracEasy isi dikkat se nikla: clients, team aur deadlines ek jagah, taaki firm kisi ek aadmi par nirbhar na rahe.
